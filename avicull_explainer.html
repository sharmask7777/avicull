The Avian Photo Crisis & The Zero-Byte Solution

Why bird photographers are trapped between chronological trip archives and taxonomic species catalogs—and how avicull solves it without duplicating a single megabyte.

🔭 The Bird Photographer's Reality

Field Workflow
You return from Keoladeo, Costa Rica, or Leh with 10,000 photos. You shot at 20–30 frames per second on continuous burst mode. You have massive RAW files (.CR3, .ARW, .NEF) paired with Lightroom .XMP sidecar edits.
  • The Catch-22: You want to browse by trip ("What did I see in Leh?") AND by species ("Show me all my Painted Stork shots!").
  • The Old Painful Way: Either manually tag every single frame (hours of tedious work), or copy files into species folders (instantly doubling disk usage and wasting 500GB+).

⚡ The Systems & AI Architecture

Design Invariants
avicull is a local-first, deterministic command-line system written in Rust specifically targeting macOS on Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4).
  • Strict Source Immutability: The input trip directory (--input) is strictly read-only. Source files are never moved, renamed, or modified.
  • APFS Zero-Byte Hard Links: Projects files into species folders using POSIX hard links (std::fs::hard_link), pointing to the identical disk inode.
  • Neural Pipeline: Multi-stage YOLO11m detector + BioCLIP 2 vision embeddings + SINR spatio-temporal Bayesian prior.

The APFS Inode Magic: Zero-Byte Projections

How POSIX hard links allow your photos to live in two (or three!) places at once without eating up any additional storage space.

🔭 For the Photographer

Storage Freedom
Normally, duplicating a 50MB Sony or Canon RAW file into another folder costs another 50MB. With avicull, opening the photo from Trips/Bharatpur/DSC_4011.CR2 or from birds_hierarchy/Painted_Stork/DSC_4011_0.96.CR2 opens the exact same physical bits on your Mac's SSD!
  • Edits in your original .XMP sidecar stay automatically connected.
  • You can even organize an individual trip folder and your entire photo archive simultaneously!

⚡ Under The Hood: POSIX Inodes

APFS & Inode Math
APFS implements directory entries as pointers to inode metadata records:
  • std::fs::hard_link(src, dst) creates an additional directory entry sharing st_ino.
  • The inode's reference counter st_nlink increments from 1 to 2 (or 3 in multi-level hierarchies). Disk payload allocated: 0 bytes.
  • Preflight Safety: avicull checks st_dev upfront. If input and output are on different volumes, it halts before running AI inference to avoid mid-run EXDEV errors.

Stage 1 & 2: Discovery, RAW Previews & Location Fallbacks

Scanning large photo trees in milliseconds, bundling sidecars, extracting embedded previews, and inferring coordinates without manual tagging.

🔭 Camera Bodies & Forgotten GPS

Real Fieldwork
Professional birding cameras (Canon EOS R5, Sony A1, Nikon Z9) often have GPS turned off to preserve battery, or lose signal under dense canopy.
  • Sibling Propagation: If only one photo in your entire trip folder has GPS (like a quick phone snap), avicull automatically propagates those coordinates to all other photos in that trip!
  • Folder Gazetteer: If your folder is named 2025-11_Keoladeo_Bharatpur, avicull recognizes the bird sanctuary name and applies its coordinates automatically!

⚡ Low-Level Parsing & BLAKE3 Bundling

High Performance I/O
src/scanner.rs and src/geo.rs prioritize high-throughput parallel execution:
  • Recursive Scanner: Uses walkdir while skipping birds_hierarchy, .avicull, and Apple Photos .photoslibrary packages to avoid infinite recursion.
  • In-Memory RAW Preview: Scans the raw binary for embedded JPEG markers (0xFFD8..0xFFD9). This extracts 4K JPEG previews in 15ms—10x faster than dcraw or full raw demosaicing.
  • AssetBundle Contract: Bundles primary RAW, companion JPEG, and sidecars (.xmp, .dop, .pp3) under a deterministic BLAKE3 hash.

Scene 4 of 8 • Detection ⏱ ~75 sec audio

Stage 3: Ultralytics YOLO11 & 15% Context Padding

Why background clutter confuses species classifiers, and how hardware-accelerated detection isolates the bird before taxonomic identification.

🔭 Field Marks & Heavy Foliage

Birding Nuance
Birds rarely pose in clean open skies. They lurk behind reed beds, perch on messy branches, or occupy only 5% of your frame.
  • If you feed the whole image to an AI, it might classify the tree or lake instead of the bird!
  • The 15% Context Padding: Tight bounding boxes often cut off the bird's beak tip, crest feathers, or tail shape—critical diagnostic field marks. avicull intelligently expands the crop by 15% on all four sides.
  • If no bird is present (empty branch / missed flight shot), it routes to _No_Bird_Detected.

⚡ CoreML Acceleration & Heuristics

ONNX on Apple Neural Engine
src/detector.rs executes Ultralytics YOLO11m (COCO class 14) via ort on Apple Silicon Metal/ANE:
  • Target Selection Policy: When multiple birds are detected, it chooses the box maximizing:
    Score = Confidence × Area
    This reliably locks onto the foreground subject rather than background flocks.
  • Clamped Resampling: The 15% padding is safely clamped to image boundaries, followed by high-quality Lanczos3 resampling to 224x224.

Scene 5 of 8 • AI & Bayesian Math ⏱ ~85 sec audio

Stage 4: BioCLIP 2 Visuals + SINR Spatio-Temporal Prior

Why pure visual AI models hallucinate on lookalikes—and how Bayesian geographical priors eliminate out-of-range false positives.

🔭 The "Lookalike" Dilemma

Merlin AI Parallel
Cornell's Merlin Bird ID is world-famous because it asks: "Where and when was this photo taken?"
  • A Blyth's Reed Warbler in India looks almost indistinguishable from a European Reed Warbler in Germany.
  • Without location, a pure vision model might guess 50/50 and assign your Indian photo to a European species!
  • With avicull, the AI knows that European Reed Warblers are statistically non-existent in Keoladeo in November. The lookalike is suppressed, and the native bird surges to 98% confidence!

⚡ The Bayesian Math: BioCLIP + SINR

Probabilistic Formulation
We fuse multimodal embeddings with neural range estimation (ICML 2023):
  • BioCLIP 2: Computes $L_2$-normalized image vector $v \in \mathbb{R}^d$ and temperature-scaled logits over precomputed taxonomic text matrix $T$:
    P_vis(s | I) = softmax(τ · v T^T)_s
  • SINR Range Prior: Evaluates $P_geo(s | lat, lon)$. Rare vagrants are kept possible via floor $\epsilon = 0.01$:
    P_post(s) ∝ P_vis(s) · max(P_geo(s), ε)^α

Scene 6 of 8 • Burst Engine ⏱ ~80 sec audio

Stage 5: Burst Consensus Smoothing (Anti-Flicker)

High-speed motor drives capture 10 to 30 frames a second. Here is how avicull guarantees an entire burst stays together without mid-sequence flickering.

🔭 High-Speed Motor Drives (20 FPS)

Camera Science
When tracking a bird in flight, your camera fires off a burst of 5 to 20 shots:
  • Frame 1 is crisp and clear. Frame 3 might have wing motion blur. Frame 4 might have a brief autofocus hunt.
  • The Annoyance: Without consensus smoothing, Frame 3 might fall below confidence or be misidentified, splitting your burst across multiple folders!
  • avicull pools the whole burst together. The sharpest frame carries the blurred frames, keeping your sequence intact.

⚡ Cosine Similarity Graph Clustering

src/burst.rs Algorithm
Frames are clustered into a burst using two strict conditions:
  • Temporal Proximity: $|\Delta t| \le 3000\text{ ms}$.
  • Visual Cosine Similarity: $v_i \cdot v_j \ge 0.80$. This prevents errors when a photographer rapidly pans to a different species!
  • Confidence-Weighted Pooling:
    P_burst(s) = (∑ w_m · P_post_m(s)) / (∑ w_m), where w_m = det_conf_m

Scene 7 of 8 • Storage & DB ⏱ ~75 sec audio

Stage 6: Routing Tiers, Collisions & Instant Relink

Deterministic naming, review tiers, cross-trip collision resolution, and sub-second threshold relinking without re-running AI.

🔭 Three Dedicated Folders

Curator's Dream
Your photos are cleanly organized into 3 transparent tiers:
  • Confirmed Species: (Score $\ge 0.65$): e.g. birds_hierarchy/Painted_Stork/DSC_4011_0.96.CR2. High confidence keepers.
  • _Needs_Review: (Score $< 0.65$): Tucked inside _Needs_Review/ for your manual review so your main species folders stay clean.
  • _No_Bird_Detected: Empty shots, scenery, or accidental clicks.
  • Instant Relink: Decide you only want $\ge 0.80$ keepers? Run relink and your tree reorganizes instantly!

⚡ SQLite State & Collision Safety

Transactional DB
All operations are backed by .avicull/manifest.sqlite:
  • Cross-Trip Collision Handling: If two different cameras or trips named a file DSC_4011.JPG, avicull detects differing inodes and prefixes the trip: Leh__DSC_4011_0.94.JPG.
  • Idempotency: Re-running organize skips existing inodes with zero duplicate work.
  • Instant Relink: Reads cached predictions from SQLite and executes filesystem unlink() and hard_link() in milliseconds without loading neural nets.

How to Use avicull: CLI & Complete Workflow

Building the binary, exporting models, running your first organization, generating reports, and rolling back safely.

🔭 3 Simple Commands for Photographers

Effortless
You don't need to be a developer to run avicull:
  1. Scan: avicull scan -i /Volumes/Photos/Leh (dry run inspection)
  2. Organize: avicull organize -i /Volumes/Photos/Leh (creates birds_hierarchy)
  3. Report: avicull report -i /Volumes/Photos/Leh (see your species checklist!)

⚡ Tunable Parameters for Power Users

High Customizability
Configure every stage of the pipeline to match your hardware and goals:
  • --min-confidence <FLOAT> (default: 0.65)
  • --burst-window-ms <INT> (default: 3000 ms)
  • --geo-prior-weight <0.0..2.0> (default: 1.0)
  • --confidence-mode [burst-consensus | per-frame]
  • --threads <INT> (Rayon thread pool allocation)
