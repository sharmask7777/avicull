Scene 2 of 9 • Filesystem Strategies & Portability ⏱ ~85 sec audio

In-Place Universal Renaming vs APFS Hard Links & The exFAT Trap

Why in-place renaming works universally on any travel drive (exFAT, FAT32, APFS, NTFS), how POSIX hard links enable zero-byte hierarchy trees, and how to avoid disk duplication traps.

🔭 For the Photographer

Storage & Portability
Normally, copying a 50MB Sony or Canon RAW file into species folders costs another 50MB. avicull offers two distinct, storage-safe strategies:
  • In-Place Renaming (Universal Default): Renames files directly where they live (<Stem>_<Category>_<Subject>...) while keeping .XMP and .DOP edits synchronized. Works on any portable SSD or SD card, including exFAT!
  • Zero-Byte Hierarchy Tree: On APFS or NTFS drives, projects files into birds_hierarchy/ via POSIX hard links. Opening the file from either folder opens the exact same physical bits!
  • ⚠️ The exFAT Trap: Portable SSDs (SanDisk Extreme, Samsung T7) are typically pre-formatted as exFAT, which does not support hard links. Drag-and-drop copying hardlink trees to exFAT duplicates all payloads. Use in-place mode on exFAT, or format the drive as APFS or NTFS (with rsync -avH)!

⚡ Under The Hood: Inodes, VFS & NTFS

VFS & Filesystem Internals
Hard links are a universal POSIX VFS concept (link(2)), not unique to APFS:
  • UNIX Extensibility: ext4, Btrfs, XFS, ZFS, F2FS, and UFS all implement directory entries as mappings to an inode number (st_ino), incrementing st_nlink with 0 additional disk payload.
  • NTFS Support: Windows NTFS natively supports hard links via Master File Table (MFT) record reference counts (up to 1,024 links per file).
  • In-Place Mode (Filesystem Agnostic): Renames parent and sidecar dentries atomically via std::fs::rename, bypassing hard link limitations on non-POSIX filesystems.
  • The Copy Trap: Standard cp -r or macOS Finder reads each dentry as a new file, inflating links into duplicates. To preserve zero-byte links across drives, use rsync -avH or pipe tar.

Stage 3: Ultralytics YOLO11 Subject Localization & 15% Padding

Why background clutter confuses species classifiers, and how hardware-accelerated localization isolates birds, mammals, and insects while preserving diagnostic field marks.

🔭 Field Marks & Heavy Foliage

Wildlife Nuance
Animals rarely pose in clean open skies. Birds lurk behind reed beds; snow leopards camouflage against scree; insects perch on flower petals.
  • If you feed the whole image to an AI, it might classify the tree or rock instead of the subject!
  • The 15% Context Padding: Tight bounding boxes often cut off beak tips, crest feathers, horn curves, or tail shapes—critical diagnostic field marks. avicull intelligently expands the crop by 15% on all four sides.
  • If no subject is present (missed flight shot or landscape), it routes cleanly to No_Subject_Detected (preserving _No_Bird_Detected compatibility).

⚡ CoreML Acceleration & Heuristics

ONNX on Apple Neural Engine
src/detector.rs executes Ultralytics YOLO11m via ort on Apple Silicon Metal/ANE:
  • Multi-Subject Targeting: When multiple candidates appear, it chooses the box maximizing:
    Score = Confidence × Area
    This reliably locks onto the foreground subject rather than background flocks.
  • Clamped Resampling: The 15% padding is safely clamped to image boundaries, followed by high-quality Lanczos3 resampling to 224x224.

Scene 5 of 9 • AI & Bayesian Math ⏱ ~85 sec audio

Stage 4: BioCLIP 2, Curated Multi-Taxa & Bayesian Spatial-Temporal Priors

Why pure visual AI models hallucinate on lookalikes—and how Bayesian geographical priors and eBird/iNaturalist checklists eliminate out-of-range false positives across 11,167 birds and curated wildlife.

🔭 Lookalikes & Global Multi-Taxa

Merlin AI Parallel
Cornell's Merlin Bird ID is world-famous because it asks: "Where and when was this photo taken?"
  • A Blyth's Reed Warbler in India looks almost indistinguishable from a European Reed Warbler in Germany.
  • Without location, a pure vision model might guess 50/50 and assign your Indian photo to a European species!
  • With avicull, the AI checks eBird regional checklists: European Reed Warblers are statistically non-existent in Keoladeo in November. The lookalike is suppressed, and the native bird surges to 98% confidence!
  • Multi-Taxa Biodiversity: Mammals (Snow Leopard, Bengal Tiger, Asian Elephant), insects, reptiles, and scenery are recognized via curated global taxonomy without inappropriate bird checklist penalties.

⚡ The Bayesian Math: BioCLIP + Checklist Prior

Probabilistic Formulation
We fuse multimodal vision embeddings with neural range maps and administrative checklists across 11,167 avian species and 224+ curated multi-taxa species:
  • BioCLIP 2: Computes $L_2$-normalized image vector $v \in \mathbb{R}^d$ and temperature-scaled logits over precomputed taxonomic text matrix $T$:
    P_vis(s | I) = softmax(τ · v T^T)_s
  • Bayesian Geo & Checklist Prior: Evaluates $P_geo(s | lat, lon, week)$. Rare vagrants are kept possible via floor $\epsilon = 0.001$:
    P_post(s) ∝ P_vis(s) · max(P_geo(s), ε)^α
  • Non-avian species bypass avian checklist multipliers, ensuring accurate classification across all 9 photographic categories.

Scene 6 of 9 • Burst Engine ⏱ ~80 sec audio

Stage 5: Burst Consensus Smoothing (Anti-Flicker)

High-speed motor drives capture 10 to 30 frames a second. Here is how avicull guarantees an entire burst stays together without mid-sequence flickering.

🔭 High-Speed Motor Drives (20 FPS)

Camera Science
When tracking a bird in flight, your camera fires off a burst of 5 to 20 shots:
  • Frame 1 is crisp and clear. Frame 3 might have wing motion blur. Frame 4 might have a brief autofocus hunt.
  • The Annoyance: Without consensus smoothing, Frame 3 might fall below confidence or be misidentified, splitting your burst across multiple folders!
  • avicull pools the whole burst together. The sharpest frame carries the blurred frames, keeping your sequence intact.

⚡ Cosine Similarity Graph Clustering

src/burst.rs Algorithm
Frames are clustered into a burst using two strict conditions:
  • Temporal Proximity: $|\Delta t| \le 3000\text{ ms}$.
  • Visual Cosine Similarity: $v_i \cdot v_j \ge 0.80$. This prevents errors when a photographer rapidly pans to a different species!
  • Confidence-Weighted Pooling:
    P_burst(s) = (∑ w_m · P_post_m(s)) / (∑ w_m), where w_m = det_conf_m

Stage 6: In-Place Renaming, SQLite Two-Phase Manifest & Cache Eviction

Deterministic in-place naming standards, synchronized companion sidecars, transactional SQLite safety, and automatic cloud cache eviction.

🔭 Rich In-Place Stems & Sidecars

Curator's Dream
Your photos are cleanly organized according to your chosen mode:
  • In-Place Standard (Default): Renames your files directly in place with rich, readable stems:
    <Stem>_<Category>_<Subject>[_<YYYYMMDD>][_<Location_Slug>].<ext>
    Example: DSC_4011_Bird_Painted_Stork_20251114_Bharatpur.CR2
  • Sidecar Parity: Companion JPEGs, Lightroom .CR2.xmp, DxO .dop, and live videos .mov receive identical stems!
  • Hierarchy Tiers (Optional): If --mode hierarchy is selected, files link to birds_hierarchy/ under Confirmed, Needs Review, or No Subject Detected.

⚡ SQLite Transactions & Cache Eviction

Transactional DB
All operations are backed by .avicull/manifest.sqlite:
  • Two-Phase Commit Safety: SQLite WAL mode records bundle hashes, GPS coordinates, bounding boxes, predictions, and filesystem operations before committing. Crashes cannot corrupt state!
  • Automated Cache Eviction: Passing --max-cache-size 5GB automatically monitors disk usage and purges oldest cached preview tiles while safely retaining prediction records.
  • Sub-Second Relink: Reads cached predictions from SQLite and executes filesystem unlink() and hard_link() in milliseconds without loading neural nets.

How to Use avicull: CLI & Complete Workflow

Building the binary, managing model weights, in-place organizing, hard-link hierarchies, catalog corrections, and safe rollback.

🔭 Everyday Commands for Photographers

Effortless
You don't need to be a developer to run avicull:
  1. Scan: avicull scan -i /Volumes/Photos/Leh (dry run inspection)
  2. Organize (In-Place): avicull organize -i /Volumes/Photos/Leh (renames in-place & generates catalog.html)
  3. Hierarchy Tree: avicull organize -i /Volumes/Photos/Leh --mode hierarchy (creates zero-byte tree)
  4. Force Recompute: avicull organize -i /Volumes/Photos/Leh --force (re-run all photos)
  5. Apply Corrections: avicull correct -i /Volumes/Photos/Leh --bundle <ID> --species "Snow_Leopard"
  6. Report: avicull report -i /Volumes/Photos/Leh (checklist across all 9 categories!)
  7. Safe Clean: avicull clean -i /Volumes/Photos/Leh (reverts changes safely)

⚡ Complete Subcommand Suite

High Customizability
Fine-tune every parameter to match your workflow and storage:
  • --mode [in-place | hierarchy] (default: in-place)
  • --max-cache-size <SIZE> (e.g. 5GB cloud cache eviction)
  • avicull correct --bundle <ID> --species <NAME>
  • avicull organize --force (bypass SQLite cache and recompute)
  • --min-confidence <FLOAT> (default: 0.65)
  • --geo-prior-weight <0.0..2.0> (default: 1.0)
  • --threads <INT> (Rayon thread pool allocation)
