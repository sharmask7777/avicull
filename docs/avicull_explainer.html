    Scene 2 • Organization Modes & Filesystem Invariants

    Three Modes: Read-Only Cataloging, Cosmetic Rename & Zero-Byte Hard Links

    --mode in-place is the safe read-only default. Opt into in-place-rename for descriptive filenames or hierarchy for POSIX hard-linked species folders.

    1. --mode in-placeDEFAULT

    Strictly read-only on your source photos and sidecars.

    • •
      Zero files moved, renamed, or deleted in --input.
    • •
      Writes only to avicull_catalog_files/ and catalog.html.
    • •
      Works on APFS, exFAT, NTFS, SMB/NFS, and cloud drives.

    2. --mode in-place-renameOPT-IN

    Cosmetic, journaled renaming of primary + sidecar pairs.

    • •
      Pattern: <Stem>_<Category>_<Subject>[_YYYYMMDD][_Loc].ext
    • •
      Transactionally journaled in manifest.sqlite.
    • •
      Lossless rollback via avicull clean.

    3. --mode hierarchyOPT-IN

    Zero-byte POSIX hard links into birds_hierarchy/.

    • •
      std::fs::hard_link shares identical disk inodes (dst_ino == src_ino).
    • •
      Supports simultaneous trip + parent hierarchies (st_nlink ≥ 2).
    • •
      Local POSIX/APFS/NTFS volumes only (same st_dev).

    macOS APFS / HFS+

    Native hard links & SF_DATALESS cloud flags. Trip + parent hierarchies coexist at 0 extra bytes.

    Linux ext4 / Btrfs / XFS / ZFS

    Full POSIX link(2) support within the same mount (st_dev).

    Windows NTFS

    MFT hard links supported. Copy with rsync -avH or POSIX tar (Finder/cp -r duplicates bytes).

    exFAT / FAT32 (The SSD Trap)

    No inode link counts. Never copy birds_hierarchy to exFAT; use default --mode in-place instead.

    Scene 3 • Stage 1 & 2: Discovery, Cloud/NAS Safety & Geolocation

    Dataless Cloud Protection, <2ms NAS Probes & 4-Tier Geo Cascade

    Before touching pixels, avicull guards against accidental cloud downloads, skips unchanged NAS trips in milliseconds, and resolves coordinates.

    🔭Cloud & DSLR Location Comfort

    Zero Accidental Cloud Downloads

    • •
      Works with Cloud-Only Libraries: iCloud, Google Drive, Proton Drive, OneDrive, and Dropbox files stay cloud-only until their bounded --max-cache-size 5GB batch runs, then newly hydrated stubs are evicted back.
    • •
      Pre-Existing Local Files Stay Local: Any photo you marked "Keep Offline" is tracked via pre_existing_local and is never evicted.
    • •
      No Camera GPS? No Problem: Folder names like 2025-11_Bharatpur_Keoladeo match the offline GazetteerEngine via trigram fuzzy matching.
    ⚡Systems Engineering Mechanics

    Dataless Guard, Depth-8 Probe & Geo Cascade

    • •
      DatalessFaultGuard: src/geo.rs & src/storage/local.rs inspect BSD meta.st_flags() & 0x4000_0000 (SF_DATALESS) before opening descriptors.
    • •
      DirectoryOnlySignatureProbe: src/scanner/probe.rs hashes directory-only (max_mtime, sum(readdir_count)) down to depth 8 in <2ms, skipping unchanged SMB/NFS trip folders.
    • •
      GeolocationNormalizer: Parses decimal coords, DMS, cardinal N/S/E/W, Google Maps URLs, and swaps inverted (lon, lat) when |lon| > 90°.
    Strict Location Precedence Cascade (src/location/resolver.rs) Highest → Lowest Priority

    1. EXIF GPSIMMUTABLE

    Hardware GPS tags embedded in RAW/JPEG are never overwritten by folder or CLI overrides.

    2. Image OverrideSQLITE

    Per-file override via avicull location --file ... or catalog.html selection.

    3. Subdir OverrideDEEPEST

    Deepest matching subfolder location override or gazetteer folder-token match.

    4. Parent / DefaultFALLBACK

    Parent trip override or --default-lat / --default-lon when unlocated.

    Scene 4 • Stage 3: Subject Detection & Relevance Floor

    Ultralytics YOLO11, 15% Context Padding & Hallucination Rejection

    scripts/infer.py isolates the primary subject with 15% padding, then guards against rock/water/statue false positives before species classification.

    🔭Subject Focus

    Separating Birds from Busy Foliage

    • •
      Primary Subject Selection: When flocks or clutter appear, YOLO11 selects the detection maximizing confidence × area.
    • •
      15% Context Halo: Expands the bounding box by 15% on each side so wingtips, tail feathers, and bills aren't clipped.
    • •
      No Ghost Birds on Rocks: If a rock or branch triggers a weak box, the visual relevance floor rejects it and classifies the full frame as Scenery or No Subject.
    ⚡Detector & Floor Mechanics

    YOLO11n (imgsz=640) + BioCLIP Relevance Gate

    • •
      Class Filter: Filters COCO_ANIMAL_CLASS_IDS (person 0, bird 14, mammals 15..=23) with conf ≥ 0.25 and area_ratio ≥ 0.001.
    • •
      Visual Relevance Floor: Rejects crops with peak_crop_sim < 0.23 (or det_conf < 0.35 and peak_crop_sim < 0.25).
    • •
      Genre Fallback: Rejected crops fall back to full-frame Scenery, Architecture, Abstract (with visual_entropy ≥ 1.0), or No_Subject_Detected.

    Scene 5 • Stage 4: BioCLIP & Bayesian Spatial-Temporal Priors

    512-d BioCLIP Embeddings + Regional Checklist Priors

    Visual similarity across 11,167 eBird species & non-avian taxa fused with regional occurrence priors—plus an override guard for rare vagrants.

    🔭Solving Lookalikes

    Separating Identical Plumage by Geography

    • •
      Lookalike Disambiguation: Visually similar species (e.g., Blyth's vs. European Reed Warbler) are cleanly separated when regional eBird checklists are applied.
    • •
      Vagrants & Zoos Protected: An unmistakable visual match (e.g., a Macaw photographed in a zoo) is never erased by a local checklist.
    ⚡Scoring Math

    Temperature-Scaled Logits + Soft-Bounded Priors

    • •
      L₂-Normalized 512-d Vector: BioCLIP (hf-hub:imageomics/bioclip) encodes crops into v ∈ ℝ⁵¹², scored via softmax(100.0 × v · Eᵀ) against [11167, 512] embeddings.
    • •
      Checklist Multiplier: In-region species receive 1.0; out-of-region species are attenuated (0.05 in infer.py, 0.001 in src/classifier.rs) scaled by --geo-prior-weight.
    • •
      Strong-Visual-Override Guard: Bypassed when top_vis_prob ≥ 0.15 and top_vis_prob > 5.0 × max_in_region_prob.

    Scene 6 • Stage 5: Burst Consensus & Fast Reclassification

    Anti-Flicker Burst Pooling & Millisecond Software Re-Scoring

    Consecutive burst frames pool their posteriors, and stored 512-d SQLite embeddings allow instant threshold re-runs without touching neural models.

    🔭Burst Stability

    Consistent Species Across 20-fps Bursts

    • •
      No Split Bursts: If frame 3 of a 5-shot kingfisher burst has motion blur, the other 4 sharp frames carry the entire burst to the same species.
    • •
      Instant Threshold Tweaks: Raising --min-confidence from 0.65 to 0.85 re-evaluates 50,000 photos in milliseconds using cached embeddings.
    ⚡Clustering & Reclassification

    src/burst.rs & --reclassify-mode fast

    • •
      Burst Gate: Same trip & category, |Δt| ≤ 3000 ms (--burst-window-ms), and embedding cosine similarity cos(vᵢ, vⱼ) ≥ 0.80.
    • •
      Confidence-Weighted Pooling: Posteriors weighted by max(0.01, det_conf) route to ConfirmedSpecies, NeedsReview, or NoBirdDetected.
    • •
      Default --reclassify-mode fast: reclassify_from_cached_embeddings reads 2,048-byte 512-d blobs from SQLite (use forced to re-run YOLO11 + BioCLIP).
    Scene 7 • Stage 6: SQLite Manifest, Cloud Eviction & Batch Rollup

    Portable avicull_catalog_files/manifest.sqlite & Safe Eviction Ordering

    Self-contained SQLite state travels with your archive across machines, journals optional renames, and aggregates multi-trip folders with zero network RAW reads.

    🔭Portable & Multi-Trip

    Move Drives Between Macs Without Re-Scanning

    • •
      Self-Contained Folder: Every trip stores its database and JPEG previews in avicull_catalog_files/ (with .avicull/ backward compatibility).
    • •
      Permanent Human Overrides: Manual corrections via CLI, catalog.html, Finder moves, or manual renames are locked with is_user_override = 1.
    • •
      Parent Archive Rollup: avicull organize-all organizes each trip folder and builds a unified root catalog.html.
    ⚡Persistence & Cloud Ordering

    src/db.rs Schema & Eviction-Before-Rename

    • •
      Core Tables: runs, bundles, bundle_decisions (with 512-d embeddings), bundle_thumbnails, hardlink_operations, directory_locations, bundle_locations, directory_sync_state, parent_child_sync.
    • •
      Eviction-Before-Rename: For cloud stubs in --mode in-place-rename: (1) classify & extract preview, (2) evict newly hydrated blocks (SF_DATALESS), (3) rename stub cleanly to avoid FileProvider sync lockouts.

    DecoupledViewProjection & DualThumbnailStrategy

    • •
      ThumbnailEmbeddingPolicy::Auto: Single-trip catalogs (≤ 2,500 items) embed base64 micro-thumbnails; parent rollups & archives > 2,500 items suppress base64 and link to avicull_catalog_files/previews/ with lazy loading (keeping HTML < 30 MB).

      ClientEvictionRemediation: Distinguishes item.is_local === false (SF_DATALESS) from missing previews, generating head -c 1 "$filepath" > /dev/null 2>&1 and materialize_evicted.sh.
